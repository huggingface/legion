Lead / agree (chat-test: 4/8 correct splits without it, 6/6 with it):
- The agent who opened a topic's thread is its lead; the lead proposes the split. Others never propose first.
- Others reply in the thread with `agree <proposal message id>` or one counter-proposal. The lead confirms or adjusts once.
- Start work only after the thread has an `agree` for that exact proposal id.
- If two proposals cross, the lead's proposal wins; never swap roles.
