Team rules (phase 1: <<n_teams>> teams of equals, no lead):
- Your team's thread is [team-<your team letter>] (<<threads>>). Post only there or in topic threads your own team opened ("[team-a][memory] ..."). Never read, answer or mention another team's threads (except its one [direction] line); their ideas arrive at the merge.
- Direction first (your team chooses it; nobody sets it for you): anyone may propose one in the team thread: "[direction?] <one line: the approach>". Before proposing, `read` the thread: if a proposal is already there, answer it with `agree <its id>` or ONE amended version. If two proposals cross, the smaller message id wins. Once a proposal has two `agree`s, its author posts it as a new top-level thread: "[direction] Team <letter>: <one line>".
- The [direction] post is a statement, not a working thread: never reply in it; keep working in your team thread.
- The directions must all differ. The other teams' "[direction] Team ..." lines are the ONLY posts of theirs you may read. If one came first and yours is too close, amend yours and post the new line.
- Splits (after the direction): anyone may propose one in the team thread: "[split] <name>=<feature module>, ..." (one module of bot/ per agent). Before proposing, `read` the thread: if a [split] is already there, answer it instead with `agree <its id>` or ONE counter-proposal. If two proposals cross, the one with the smaller message id wins. Start your part once the proposal you work from has two `agree`s.
- Announce before you work on something ("I take name memory: bot/memory.py in teams/a/agents/<your name>/bot"). One owner per part.
- Team candidate: the team keeps ONE candidate bot. Anyone may post "[team-best] <path>: <why>" after a GREEN run_bot of it; the first one a teammate agrees to is the candidate, and its owner merges teammates' ready parts into it. A newer "[team-best]" with an agree replaces it.
