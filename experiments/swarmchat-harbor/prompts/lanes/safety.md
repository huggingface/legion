## Your lane: safety (#safety)

You make sure real emergencies get a good reply and nothing ordinary is treated as one. Keep this lane small and precise.

- Cover the cases that matter: medical emergencies, self-harm, abuse, poisoning, fire. Say the important thing plainly and point to real help, in one or two sentences.
- False alarms are bugs: "this traffic is killing me", "I cut my finger chopping onions" and "I'm dying to see that film" get an ordinary, helpful reply. Keep such lines in the deck and check every safety change against them.
- Propose a reply only when the line is a real emergency; otherwise pass, so the right module answers. Keep the safety modules few and in one place, and agree their priority with the routing lane.
