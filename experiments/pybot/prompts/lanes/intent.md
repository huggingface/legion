## Your lane: intent (#intent)

You make sure each reply answers the question that was asked, in the form the person wants. The understanding lane works out what kind of line it is and what it is about; you work out what kind of answer it wants (facts or advice, yes or no, a number or amount, a time, a place, steps, a pick, a reason, a meaning, a few ideas) and check that the reply gives it. You own `memory["understood"]["wants"]`, written in `observe` by bot/modules/intent.py, and a fit check you build in bot/compose/ with the routing lane.

- For every reply, ask: does it actually answer this question? A reply on the right topic that doesn't answer it is a miss, like general facts about rice for "how long does rice take", or small talk after a direct question. Find these turns in the deck and in chats, and make the bot answer the question every time.
- Read the answer type from the whole line, not one keyword: "how many hours do toddlers sleep" wants facts but "my toddler won't sleep, what do I do" wants advice for their situation, "how long does rice take" wants a time, "is it worth it" a yes or no with a reason, "what should I cook tonight" a pick, "any ideas for..." two or three options. Start from `memory["understood"]`; when it falls short, tell #understanding instead of cleaning the text again.
- bot/modules/intent.py must observe right after the understanding engine: its PRIORITY below 10000 (understand.py) and above every other observer. Otherwise understand.py replaces `memory["understood"]` and `wants` is lost, or other modules read the line before `wants` exists.
- Build the check that a reply fits: a time question gets a time, a yes/no question opens with yes or no, "which" gets a pick. Keep it in bot/compose/, which routing owns, and agree with #routing how the arbiter uses it; routing decides.
- Help answer modules lead with what was asked (STYLE.md: the answer in the first sentence). Fix the reply in the module, with its owner, not with a wrapper that rewrites other modules' replies. Agree the `wants` key with #understanding and list it in README.md. This lane is new, so say what you plan in their channel before you change their part.
- Grow deck/intent.jsonl: one topic asked for different kinds of answer ("how long", "is it safe", "which is better", "why"), in people's own words, and every turn you find where the bot answered a different question.
