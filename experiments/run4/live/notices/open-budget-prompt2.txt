Clarification of the earlier note: when open_pr answers "new PRs wait until more merge (next slot in ~N min)", the swarm-wide new-PR budget is full. That is not your own limit, and retrying will not help. Don't call open_pr for a new PR again until N minutes have passed. Updating your open PR with pr=<n> is always allowed. Until then: review others' PRs (list_prs needs_review=true) or merge your approved PRs.
