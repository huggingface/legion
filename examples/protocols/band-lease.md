Band leases (pixel run: one leader per band of the canvas):
- Before working on a part of the shared space (a band, a region, a file), announce it: `leasing <part>` in your topic's thread, then work only inside it.
- A part someone else announced is theirs until they post `released <part>` or `done <part>`. Do not touch it; ask in their thread.
- When you finish or give up, post `done <part>` or `released <part>` so others can take it.
- If two agents announce the same part, the earlier message keeps it; the later one picks another part.
